# Design

## Context

The hero, journey and audit lens already use a shared demonstration dataset. The video panel interrupts the path between them.

## Goals / Non-Goals

Give the visitor a legible route from a group total to one report entry. The sample represents Üretim A.Ş. in March 2026. Hosting and customer data are outside the local preview.

## Decisions

Use the product greens, proportional logo blocks, a white field and fine rules to connect subsidiaries to the total. Keep the approved heading, tighten its supporting text and reserve mono type for evidence. Lead directly from the total to the invoice example. Render the three stages as native disclosures so touch, keyboard and script-free readers can inspect them. Each stage pairs a prominent value with its source. The report stage links to the audit lens. The journey explains workflow and the platform offers a second entry to the same record.

## Risks / Trade-offs

A single invoice can be mistaken for the group result. Label both scales explicitly. A long invoice identifier may overflow on phones. Wrap metadata and test the narrow layout. Keep the sticky header clear of both fragment destinations.
